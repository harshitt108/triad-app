How it works

One small loop, every day.

  1. Morning

    Plan your three

    Write the three tasks that would make today a good day. Each one starts with sensible settings: 25 minutes and a category. Stuck? Ask for ideas, or reuse a task you've done before.

  2. During the day

    Focus on one at a time

    Swipe to a task and tap Focus. Pick 10, 25, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. Pause when life happens; your time is kept.

  3. Evening

    Finish all three

    Mark each one complete. Finish all three and your streak grows by a day. Tomorrow, you start fresh.
