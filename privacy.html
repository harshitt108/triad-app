Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

01

The short version

Triad runs on your device. Your tasks, streak, and settings never leave it — there is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for them to go to. The one thing that does leave is an anonymous crash report when the App crashes, so we can fix it. We don’t run ads, we don’t use third-party ad trackers, and we have no data of yours to sell.

02

Information we collect

Almost nothing, and never your content. Everything the App records stays on your device: your task titles, categories, durations, and priorities; your daily plan and completion history; your streak and focus-session stats; the onboarding answers you give (like your cue time or what tends to derail you) and your notification preference. Two things do leave your device. First, crash diagnostics: if the App crashes, an automatic report goes to Sentry, our error-reporting provider — the error message, the stack trace, and basic device information such as your model, OS version, and App version. It contains no task text, no plan history, and nothing identifying you personally; we have deliberately disabled the breadcrumb trail that would otherwise record what you were doing at the time. Second, if you subscribe, your subscription status (active, trialing, expired) is handled by the App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies — we never receive your card number or other payment details.

03

Information we don’t collect

We don’t collect health data, financial account data, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers — or, for that matter, your name or email, since there is no account to attach them to. We never receive your task text at all, including in crash reports.

04

How we use it

Your data is used on your device only: to compute your streak, history, and (for Pro subscribers) trends and insights, and to send the single optional daily reminder notification you can turn on or off yourself. Your subscription status is used to unlock Pro features.

05

Where your data lives

In a database file on your device, inside the App’s private storage, and nowhere else. It is protected by your device’s own security — its lock screen and app sandboxing — rather than by encryption we add on top, and we don’t claim otherwise. Anything you export with Back up data leaves that protection the moment you send it somewhere, so store those copies somewhere you trust.

06

Who we share it with

Two processors, and nobody else. Sentry receives crash diagnostics so we can fix bugs, and — if you subscribe — RevenueCat together with the Apple App Store and Google Play handle billing and subscription status. Each operates under its own privacy policy. No other service provider sees anything, because nothing else leaves your device. We don’t sell your data or share it for advertising.

07

How long we keep it

For exactly as long as you keep it. Your data persists on your device until you delete the App or use Clear local data inside it — we never see it, so we can’t retain it, and we can’t delete it on your behalf either.

08

Your rights

You already hold them directly. Your data is on your device: Back up data exports it, Export data (Pro) gives you the full task history as CSV, and Clear local data erases it permanently. Rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA generally apply to data a company holds about you — we hold none. Contact us with any question about this policy.

09

Children’s privacy

Triad isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — there is no account to create and no data reaches us.

10

Security

Keeping your data off the internet removes most of the risk, but not all of it: anyone who can unlock your device can open the App, and a backup you export is only as safe as where you put it. No method of storage is 100% secure, and we can’t guarantee absolute security.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We’ll update the date at the top of this document when we do.

12

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, export, or delete your data, can be sent to harshitcreativestudio@gmail.com.
